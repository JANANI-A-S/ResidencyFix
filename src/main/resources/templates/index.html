About ResidencyFix

Built to keep hostel life safe, clean, and well managed.

ResidencyFix helps hostel residents, caretakers, and wardens stay connected around daily issues such as plumbing, electrical faults, room maintenance, housekeeping, and common-area safety concerns.

  • Resident complaint logging for rooms, facilities, and shared spaces
  • Maintenance coordination for faster service follow-up
  • Warden oversight for recurring problems and service quality
